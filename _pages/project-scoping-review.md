<section class="project-section">
<p>One of the major challenges that autonomous cars are facing today is driving in urban environments. <strong>Autonomous vehicles need the ability to communicate with other road users, especially pedestrians, about their intention</strong>. However, the communication display of pedestrian-vehicle interaction used in autonomous vehicles are not well studied.  <strong>A scoping review was conducted <em>to understand the current state of research on pedestrian-vehicle interaction with a particular focus on on-road studies</em>.</strong></p>
</section>
