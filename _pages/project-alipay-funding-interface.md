  <div class="project-body">
<section class="project-section">
<p>In 2021, Alipay is popular all over China. On its basis service of &nbsp;fast payment, Alipay &nbsp;gradually introduce more functions, hoping to become an online wealth management platform for all of China. However, not everyone accepts the concept.</p><p>I was part of an ambitious project to redesign the fund sector for the fastest growing payments company in history.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-section">
<p><strong>Team</strong></p><p>Xin Ye, Wenmin Li, Yuqin He, Alibaba Design team.</p><p><strong>Exhibition</strong></p><p>@ 2020 Alibaba Design Showcase &amp; Competition（This project won “the Most Valuable Award” and “the First Place in the Internet Financial Management Category”.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-section">
<p><strong>Duration</strong></p><p>Jan 2020 - Apr 2020 (4 mo)</p><p><strong>Methods</strong></p><p>Prototyping; Eye-tracking; Usability test;</p>
</section>

<section class="project-section">
<h3><strong>THE CHALLENGE</strong></h3><h2><strong>PEOPLE ARE AFRAID TO USE FINANCIAL INSTRUMENT</strong></h2><h3>The truth is that the financial industry itself needs a greater degree of trust. It is professional, it involves "money", and trust breaks down very quickly if there is a risk.<br><br>More and more Chinese people have such money concept: a lifetime without financial management, afraid of loss.</h3><h2><strong>OUR GOALS</strong></h2><h3>1. Discover the influence factors of trust<br>2. Criteria for value design of trust<br>3. Iterative improvement of the existing Alipay fund sector</h3><h3></h3><h2><strong>MY ROLE</strong></h2><h3>I led the research and design of the new "Fund" section of Alipay and collaborated with two other user researchers and two designers.</h3>
</section>
